Tips for Landing Remote 3D / Houdini Work

  1. Portfolio on ArtStation is non-negotiable. Studios and recruiters search ArtStation first. Keep it current with your best 3D/motion/Houdini work.
  2. SideFX Forum is the #1 source for Houdini gigs. The Jobs subforum at sidefx.com/forum/60 has direct-from-studio posts that don't appear on LinkedIn.
  3. Set yourself "available" on Working Not Working. Studios actively reach out to talent there. Strong in motion/3D freelance.
  4. Join the Facebook groups. "Motion Design Community" and "After Effects & Motion Graphics" have the highest volume of informal "looking for a 3D designer" posts.
  5. Join the SideFX Official Discord (discord.gg/houdini). Has dedicated #jobs and #freelance channels.
  6. Tech companies pay the most. Apple, Meta, Snap, and Epic pay $133k-$313k for 3D/spatial roles. Remote options are growing.
  7. AI skills are a salary multiplier. Designers fluent in AI tools command a 15-50% premium. Worth learning.
  8. Motionographer is the industry gold standard. Subscribe to their newsletter for curated job listings from top studios.
  9. VFX studios are mostly hybrid/onsite (Vancouver, London, Montreal). For fully remote Houdini work, target freelance contracts and tech companies.
  10. Monitor Twitter/X daily. Search "hiring motion designer" or "looking for Houdini artist" for real-time leads that never make it to job boards.
